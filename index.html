A Two-Player Scoring Game

Rules of the Game
  1. Player 1 and Player 2 take turns — one ball each.
  2. Tap the ball to bowl. A page number flips on the board.
  3. The last digit of that page number is the runs scored on that ball.
  4. Each player faces 10 balls in total.
  5. The player with the higher score after 10 balls each is the Winner!
